# Code of Conduct for VizTR Meet

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a friendly and safe environment for all contributors.\n\nThis project adheres to the Contributor Covenant code of conduct.\n
## Our Standards

Examples of unacceptable behavior:\n\n* Using sexualized language or imagery\n* Posting sexually explicit or violent content\n* Making derogatory comments\n* Personal attacks\n* Unwelcome attention or compliments\n* Trolling\n* Public or private harassment\n* Publishing private information\n* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate\n
## Our Responsibilities\n\nProject maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and being role models for appropriate behavior.\n
## Attributions\n\nThis Code of Conduct is a derivative of the [Django Project Code of Conduct](https://www.djangoproject.com/foundation/community/values/) and the [PSF Code of Conduct](https://www.python.org/psf/codeofconduct/).\n\n## Enforcement\n\nInstances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers by contacting support@viztr-meet.com.\n\nWe will investigate and take action as needed.\n
